## Business Problem

### Background

* Customers need to contact customer support even for simple product orders.
* Customer support representatives manually check product availability before processing orders.
* Manual order processing increases the possibility of incorrect order details and product delivery discrepancies.
* Order tracking requires manual intervention from the customer support team.
* The process is time-consuming for both customers and customer support representatives.
* The high volume of manual activities increases the workload on the customer support team and may result in delays in order processing.

### Problem Statement

The current product ordering process relies heavily on manual intervention from the customer support team. Product ordering, availability checking, and order tracking are performed manually, resulting in increased processing time and significant manual effort for routine activities. The manual process also increases the risk of errors and discrepancies in product orders, which may lead to incorrect order fulfilment and additional customer support effort.

| - | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 | 100% of product orders are currently placed through customer support calls                                 | Ordering simple products requires significant customer support time and manual effort                                                                                                    |

| 4 | Customer support handles a high volume of manual orders during peak sales periods                          | Increased workload can result in delayed processing and missed orders                                                                                                                    |
| 5 | Customers have no centralized visibility into product availability, order status, and shipping information | Lack of transparency can increase customer dissatisfaction and negatively affect customer retention and sales                                                                            |

### Business Impact if Unresolved

* A significant number of customer support executives are required to provide manual support, which may increase operational costs and exceed the allocated budget, affecting overall profit.
* Customer dissatisfaction may increase due to the time consumed in ordering and tracking products.
* Discrepancies in product deliveries may increase, which could negatively affect the company.
* Manual work in the ordering process may cause a decline in product sales.
