## Business Objective

### Objective Statement

Design and implement an online ordering management system to enable customers to order products, check product availability, process orders, and track orders online through the portal. The objective is to reduce manual work by 90% and launch the application within two quarters.

### SMART Breakdown

| Criteria   | Detail                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Specific   | Automate product ordering, product availability checking, order processing, and delivery tracking through the online portal                               |
| Measurable | Reduce manual effort by 90% and overall discrepancies through the online portal                                                                           |
| Achievable | Achieve this through a shared product directory and product mapping, along with payment gateway integration, store details, and delivery shipment details |
| Relevant   | Enable customers to directly order products and monitor their orders, thereby improving customer satisfaction                                             |
| Time-bound | Full rollout within 2 quarters of project kick-off                                                                                                        |

### Success Metrics (KPIs)

| KPI                           | Current                        | Target                |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------ | --------------------- |
| Average Order processing time | 30 – 45 mins                   | 5 – 10 mins           |
| Product availability checking | Through customer support calls | Through online portal |
| Order tracking                | Through customer support calls | Through online portal |
| Return/Refund application     | Through customer support calls | Through online portal |
